# Code of conduct

We are committed to a respectful, harassment-free community for everyone, regardless of experience, identity, background or viewpoint.

Be constructive, assume good intent, discuss evidence rather than people, and respect privacy and licensing boundaries. Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, doxxing and deliberate disruption are not acceptable.

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and temporarily or permanently restrict participation when behavior is inappropriate. Report conduct concerns privately through the maintainer's GitHub profile; reports will be handled as confidentially as practical.

This policy follows the spirit of the [Contributor Covenant 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
